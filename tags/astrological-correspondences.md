## Court Cards

**System:** Golden Dawn — each rank corresponds to a modality (Cardinal = Queen, Fixed = Knight, Mutable = King). Pages = all three signs of the element.

### Wands (Fire) — Aries, Leo, Sagittarius

| Card | Sign | Modality |
|------|------|----------|
| Page of Wands | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | all (element) |
| Knight of Wands | Leo | fixed |
| Queen of Wands | Aries | cardinal |
| King of Wands | Sagittarius | mutable |
